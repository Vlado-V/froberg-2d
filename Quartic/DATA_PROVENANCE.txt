Certificate data for the three initial characteristic-two dimensions

Only n28, n29, and n30 are required by this submission. The original data
records below identify the inputs used for the byte-equality checks.

Each inverse.bin is stored as ordinary <=64 MiB chunks under inverse.bin.parts;
its manifest gives their concatenation order. sparse.bin is not shipped: the
Lean meta-level CertificateBinaryIO reconstructs exactly the original sparse
bytes from coefficients.bin, products.bin, selected.bin, and quadCodes.bin.
All 108,345 reconstructed rows and all three chunked inverses were compared
byte for byte with the original inputs before the packaged rebuild.

The existing proof-producing elaborators still verify the inverse equations
and their polynomial interpretation in Lean. Reconstructing bytes supplies
no unproved mathematical assumption.
